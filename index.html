About Me

Building native multimodal systems.

My interests connect representation learning, large-scale model training, and practical speech applications.

I am a master's student at Fudan University. My research focuses on multimodal foundation models and audio representation learning, especially speech tokenizers, audio tokenizers, ASR, TTS, spoken dialogue generation, and speech-to-speech models.

I am interested in building native end-to-end multimodal models that go beyond text-only intelligence, while making frontier multimodal technology useful in real downstream scenarios.
