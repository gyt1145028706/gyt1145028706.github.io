🎄 About Me

Hi! I am Yitian Gong, a master's student at Fudan University advised by Prof. Xipeng Qiu. My research interests focus on multimodal foundation models, especially speech tokenizers, audio tokenizers, audio representation learning, text-to-speech generation, ASR, and speech-to-speech systems.

I am dedicated to building native end-to-end multimodal models and making frontier speech and audio technology useful in real downstream scenarios. Recently, I have worked on the MOSS speech stack, including scalable audio tokenization, TTS models, spoken dialogue generation, and practical transcription/diarization systems.
